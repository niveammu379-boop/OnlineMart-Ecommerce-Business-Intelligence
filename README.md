#OnlineMart - E-Commerce Business Intelligence & Customer Analytics
##Project Overview
An end-to-end business intelligence and data analytics platform designed to analyze e-commerce sales performance, customer purchasing behavior, and profitability trends across diverse product categories and regional markets to drive data-backed business decisions.

##Tech Stack & Tools
**Data Extraction & Prep:** Python (Pandas, NumPy)
**Database / Querying:** SQL
**Exploratory Analysis:** Microsoft Excel
**Interactive Dashboards:** Tableau & Power BI

##Key Business Insights & Findings
Finding 1 (Top performing product category)
Finding 2 (Month-over-month sales growth)
Finding 3 (Customer churn or discount vs profit correlation)
